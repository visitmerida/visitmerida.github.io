Cenote mornings

Get to the cenotes right when they open (usually 8–9am). You'll swim in glass-clear water before the crowds — and the light through the openings is unreal. Best morning of your trip, guaranteed.

Hidden gem: Cenote Xlacah

Swim in a sacred Maya cenote right beside ancient ruins at Dzibilchaltún, 20 minutes from Mérida. Way fewer crowds than the famous ones. Who's adding it to their list? 📌

🌮

First thing to eat: cochinita pibil

Slow-roasted pork marinated in achiote, wrapped in banana leaves. Get it at a market lonchería, not a tourist restaurant. Your taste buds will thank you.

🌅

Golden hour

Golden hour in Mérida hits different: the Paseo de Montejo mansions glow, the heat breaks, and the whole city comes out to stroll. Best free show in Yucatán. What's your favorite sunset spot? 👇
